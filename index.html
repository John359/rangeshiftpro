🛡️ Am I Safe? — Spot Trader Protection

This page helps you avoid the #1 spot trader mistake: buying a coin that looks cheap but is actually in a downtrend — and getting stuck waiting weeks for it to recover.

🪤 Am I Trapped In This Coin?

Enter a coin name and the price you bought it at. The app will tell you honestly whether you're in a good position or stuck — and what your best move is.

✅ Is It Safe To Buy This Coin Right Now?

Before you buy, enter the coin name and the app will check if the price range is stable, rising, or starting to fall — so you don't buy at the wrong time.
